Be the juror: State of Ohio v. Caleb Flynn

Follow a real murder trial step by step. See how openings, witnesses, cross-examination, experts, and closing arguments work, then reach your own verdict.

Please read first. This case involves the real death of Ashley Flynn, a wife and mother. The recordings include a 911 call and body camera video of the aftermath. Discuss it with respect for her, her daughters, and everyone affected. The trial is finished, but the outcome is not shown until you have reached your own verdict, so please do not look it up.
